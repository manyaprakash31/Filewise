# Meridian Technologies - Remote Work and Equipment Policy
Document ID: POL-2026-REM
Effective Date: March 1, 2026
Version: 3.2

## Section 1: Eligibility and Work Hours
All full-time employees who have successfully completed their 90-day probationary period are eligible for remote work arrangements. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM Eastern Time, during which team members must be reachable via the internal messaging platform and email.

## Section 2: Home Office Equipment Allowance
Full-time remote employees receive a one-time home office equipment stipend of $1,200 upon hire to purchase ergonomic furniture, external monitors, and peripherals. Additionally, an annual technology refresh stipend of $400 is provided on the anniversary of the employee's start date. Equipment purchased with company funds remains company property and must be logged with the IT asset inventory within 14 business days.

## Section 3: Data Security and Network Access
Remote team members must connect to the Meridian Virtual Private Network (VPN) with multi-factor authentication (MFA) enabled whenever accessing internal company databases, source repositories, or customer data. Split-tunneling is strictly prohibited on company-issued workstations. Public Wi-Fi networks in coffee shops, airports, or hotels must never be used without an active VPN connection.

## Section 4: Expense Reimbursement and Internet
Meridian Technologies provides a monthly internet connectivity subsidy of $75 to offset high-speed residential broadband costs. To qualify for reimbursement, residential download speeds must meet or exceed 100 Mbps. Expense reimbursement requests must be submitted through the Expensify portal by the 5th business day of each calendar month.
